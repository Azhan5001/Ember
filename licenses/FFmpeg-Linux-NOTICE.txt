Ember for Linux bundles a compact build of FFmpeg and FFprobe 8.1.3.
This build is distributed under the GNU General Public License version 3.
The corresponding FFmpeg release, its checksum and build configuration are
recorded in scripts/build-linux-media.sh in the Ember source archive.
FFmpeg source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.3.tar.xz

The RPM build statically links libvpx 1.15.0, Opus 1.5.2, and x264 build 164.
Upstream sources and licenses:
https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libvpx/+/refs/tags/v1.15.0
https://opus-codec.org/
https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264/-/tree/31e19f9
The packaged tools also use the system OpenSSL, zlib and dav1d libraries.

0.7.3 enables atempo for audio speed conversion. Codec copyright notices are included alongside this notice.
