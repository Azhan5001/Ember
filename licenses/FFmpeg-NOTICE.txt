Ember's compatibility decoder uses separate FFmpeg and FFprobe executables.
They are supplied by BtbN's FFmpeg-Builds project and licensed under the
GNU General Public License. See FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt for the license text.
The scripts and source references used to build the binaries are provided by
https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds and https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg.
The matching build release and binary checksums are recorded in the source
package at scripts/ffmpeg-assets.json.
